## 1.3 Why Pappa.ai (six pillars)
*Maps to: WhyChoosePappa.jsx*

**Heading:** Built around how education actually works.

1. **One account, every role.**
   A parent who also teaches, or a teacher who also volunteers, signs in once and switches roles. No second account and no second password.

## 5.2 General
*Maps to: GeneralFAQ.jsx*

**What is Pappa.ai?**
Pappa.ai is a platform for running learning institutions. It brings safety, academics, programs, enrollment, fees, volunteers and communication into one place, with one login for everyone.

**What kinds of institutions use Pappa.ai?**
Schools, colleges, academies, training centers, event-based programs and community learning groups, from a single campus to many.

**Does it work for programs without grades or classes?**
Yes. Pappa.ai supports formal learning (grades and sections), open learning (programs and batches), or a mix of both.

**Who uses Pappa.ai day to day?**
Institution admins, branch admins, teachers, parents and guardians, students and volunteers. Each has their own view.

**How much does it cost?**
Pappa.ai is offered on a subscription, shaped around your institution's size and needs. [Contact us](/contact) for a quote.

## 5.4 Roles & access

**Do I need a separate account for each role?**
No. One account holds all your roles, and you choose which one to use when you sign in.

**I have children at two different institutions. Do I need two apps?**
No. If both institutions use Pappa.ai, you'll see both children under one login.

**Can we give someone access just for a short program?**
Yes. Access can be time-limited, for a camp, a term or a single event.

**Can each person only see what's relevant to them?**
Yes. Access depends on role, so people see only what their role needs.

## 5.5 Safety & privacy
*Maps to: SafetyPrivacyFAQ.jsx*

**How does Pappa.ai help keep children safe?**
Each child is linked to their parents or guardians and has emergency contacts on file. Drop-off and pickup are recorded on campus, and staff and families are alerted to late or unauthorized pickups.

**Are emergency contacts required?**
Yes. Every child must have emergency contacts on record.

**Can other institutions see our data?**
No. Each institution's data is kept separate from every other institution's.

**Who can see our information?**
Only people at your institution with the right role. Important actions are logged, so you can see what changed and when.

**Do you sell our data?**
No. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details.

## 5.6 Fees & enrollment

**How can families pay?**
Online, offline at the front desk, or with pay-later. All payments are recorded in one place.

**How do new learners enroll?**
Through your public enrollment page. Adults register themselves, and children are registered by a parent or guardian.

**Do you track annual registration fees?**
Yes. Registration fees are tracked year by year.
